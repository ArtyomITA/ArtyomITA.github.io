02Implementations

From the GTX 1080 to the RTX PRO 6000

Inference kernels and configurations for a GTX 1080 and for an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. Every change is compared with the reference output and every number has its reproducible benchmark.

blackwell6000-qwen3.8-flash-next

October 2026

GPU
RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell, 96 GB
Stack
vLLM · CUDA · NVFP4
Licence
Apache-2.0

Qwen3.8 Flash-Next and its Swift 1.5 fine-tune in NVFP4 on a single RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell with vLLM: 4 users with 227k tokens of context each, about 100 tok/s per user, a peak of 186.5 tok/s with one user. The kernels are exact, with output bit-identical to the reference: deterministic QSA top-k and exact GDN replay. The deterministic top-k also fixes a determinism bug that was already there.

minicpm5-kernel-pascal

MiniCPM5-2B on a GTX 1080 from 33 to 83 tok/s: slots erased when a conversation ends, Pascal kernels with bit-identical output, f16 KV cache and an FR-Spec drafter with a 32k vocabulary. Every step has its quality check.

PythonC++CUDA llama.cpp

bonsai2-ternary-pascal

Ternary Bonsai 2 9B on a GTX 1080: +51% decode (from 55.9 to 84.4 tok/s) with bit-identical output. Five CUDA changes to PrismML's llama.cpp fork; the PQ2_0 mat-vec is already in the fork (PR #368).

llama.cpp (patch)Python CUDA

llm2vec-8gb-offload-lossless-batching

LLM2Vec (Llama-3-8B) on an 8 GB GPU via RAM offload; batching without altering the embeddings: padding shifts them (cosine similarity 0.76 against the reference), the fix brings them back to 0.999956.

PythonPyTorch text encoder for Kimodo

lfm-to-kimodo

Kimodo generates human motion from a text, but it expects a vector from LLM2Vec on Llama-3-8B: 15 GB of weights and 90-190 seconds per sentence. I replaced that piece with LFM2.5-Embedding-350M and a linear map from 1024 to 4096 dimensions: 0.7 s per sentence and 700 MB. The output is npz and bvh files, to open in Blender or Unity.

PythonPyTorch Kimodo, Unity

bonsai-webgpu-tiled-vae-fix

Root cause analysis of the tiled VAE decoder of Bonsai-Image 4B in WebGPU on GPUs without shader-f16: a 1.4 KB patch that enables 512px and above on Pascal and older GPUs, with no quality loss on f16 devices.

JavaScriptWebGPUWGSL

hildanext

I convert an autoregressive LLM (Qwen3-0.6B) into a discrete diffusion model with the LLaDA 2.1 recipe, with a training and inference backend that runs on Pascal.

PythonPyTorch diffusion LM

Replicating the Cohere test on Qwen3.8

Cohere reported that Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, during a security analyst task in a sandbox, tried to upload a file to an Alibaba storage bucket. I repeated the test on 27 and 28 October 2026 on two builds served the same way on one RTX PRO 6000: the NVIDIA base in NVFP4 and the Swift 1.5 fine-tune.

The memorized URL is in the NVIDIA base and the fine-tune keeps it. It shows up when an agent keeps running into broken pages; in ordinary coding tasks neither build produced an Alibaba or metadata address. Denying egress and blocking *.aliyuncs.com and the metadata addresses in the harness covers both.

Vergilius

local-first

A personal assistant that does not talk to external services: it runs locally on a GTX 1080 (8 GB), with no API keys and no conversation leaving the machine. Default model LFM2.5-2.6B with a speculative drafter: 64 tok/s in decoding, 78-100 tok/s with the drafter on. A groundedness check compares the numbers in each answer with the tool results: unsupported numbers dropped from 3 turns out of 35 to 1 out of 36.1

05Experimental projects

A language model on a real connectome

How much of the context is held by attention, and how much by a recurrent core? Test bed: the complete connectome of the Drosophila nervous system (male-cns:v1.0, 166,700 neurons, 2.75 M synapses at threshold 10)2 as the recurrent LIF core of a language model: synaptic signs fixed by the neurotransmitter, magnitudes initialized from synapse counts, surrogate gradient, FP32 CUDA Graph. Protocol with degree-preserving null models, token-matched baselines and in-silico lesions.

2male-cns:v1.0 connectome (Janelia FlyEM · Google Research, CC-BY licence): 25.6 M connections in total, 2,753,975 above the 10-synapse threshold.

FlyToLLM

research · 2026

Text enters through the 17,937 real sensory neurons and is read out from the 2,241 descending and motor neurons; an external causal attention (4 heads, cache 128) re-enters through the same ports. Results are reported with their controls: at equal data the shuffled graph beats the anatomical wiring; the whole optic lobe can be removed with no change in cross-entropy; 4+4 depth is enough for the measured reachability (readout 2 hops from the ports).3

34+4 substeps per token, TBPTT 8, FP32 CUDA Graph: 0.65 s per update on a GTX 1080, 0.38 s with the fused kernel; 5.46 M parameters.

A note on the figures

The charts are drawn in this page with HTML, CSS and canvas, with no images. The 3D scene at the top, built with three.js, is an illustration of tokens flowing through the compute units of a GPU: it is not a measurement, and it does not start when reduced motion is on. The FlyToLLM figure is an illustration too: the dots come from a fixed seed (166700, the neuron count), so every reader sees the same drawing. The CUDA Graphs chart is a schematic; the ones on LLM2Vec, Vergilius and FantaOracle plot measured numbers. The numbers in the text come from the repositories and the PR pages.
