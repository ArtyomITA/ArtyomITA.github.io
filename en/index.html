Research on attention and memory

A language model on a real connectome

How much of the context is held by attention, and how much by a recurrent core? Test bed: the complete connectome of the Drosophila nervous system (male-cns:v1.0, 166,700 neurons, 2.75 M synapses at threshold 10)1 as the recurrent LIF core of a language model: synaptic signs fixed by the neurotransmitter, magnitudes initialized from synapse counts, surrogate gradient, FP32 CUDA Graph. Protocol with degree-preserving null models, token-matched baselines and in-silico lesions.

1male-cns:v1.0 connectome (Janelia FlyEM · Google Research, CC-BY licence): 25.6 M connections in total, 2,753,975 above the 10-synapse threshold.

FlyToLLM

research · 2026

Text enters through the 17,937 real sensory neurons and is read out from the 2,241 descending and motor neurons; an external causal attention (4 heads, cache 128) re-enters through the same ports. Results are reported with their controls: at equal data the shuffled graph beats the anatomical wiring; the whole optic lobe can be removed with no change in cross-entropy; 4+4 depth is enough for the measured reachability (readout 2 hops from the ports).2

animated pipeline and numbers

PyTorch · CUDA Graphs; LIF · surrogate gradient; TBPTT · Adam; TinyStories · BPE 4096

24+4 substeps per token, TBPTT 8, FP32 CUDA Graph: 0.65 s per update on a GTX 1080, 0.38 s with the fused kernel; 5.46 M parameters.

Local speech-to-text and privacy-first tools

Desktop applications that process audio and text on the user's machine, with no external services.

Vergilius

local-first

A personal assistant that does not talk to external services: it runs locally on a GTX 1080 (8 GB), with no API keys and no conversation leaving the machine. Default model LFM2.5-2.6B with a speculative drafter: 64 tok/s in decoding, 78-100 tok/s with the drafter on. A groundedness check compares the numbers in each answer with the tool results: unsupported numbers dropped from 3 turns out of 35 to 1 out of 36.3

3The repository includes two third-party projects as submodules, Odysseus (assistant) and ShadowBroker (open-source intelligence), under AGPL-3.0: they are dependencies, not original work.

A note on the figures

Every figure is computed in this page with hand-written canvas and SVG, with no images or libraries. The pseudo-random generators have fixed seeds (27721, the PR number, for Fig. 1 and Fig. 3; 166700, the neuron count, for Fig. 2), so every reader sees the same drawing. Scrolling is an input: in Fig. 1 it raises the sensory current, in the others it advances the figure, which rewinds if you scroll back. With reduced motion on, each figure shows a single chosen frame. Fig. 1, 2 and 3 are schematics; Fig. 4, Fig. 5 and Fig. 6 plot measured numbers; the numbers in the text, captions and Table 1 come from the repositories.
