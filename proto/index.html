03 edge AI · Meta Quest 3S

A 300 euro headset that talks

Faster than you can listen. No cloud, no PC, no cable.

LFM3-Omni-2B runs entirely inside the headset with llama.cpp: backbone on the Adreno GPU through OpenCL, depthformer on the CPU, codec on its own thread. Stock needed 1.8 s to say one second of speech. Now 0.85 s.

  • 1.17xreal time, was 0.56x
  • 1.02xon battery, was 0.33x
  • −76%memory
